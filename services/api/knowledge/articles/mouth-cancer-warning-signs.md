# Mouth cancer warning signs and screening

## What it is
Mouth (oral) cancer can affect the lips, tongue, cheeks, gums, floor of the mouth and palate. Found early, it is far more treatable. **No photo, app or AI can diagnose or rule out mouth cancer.** Diagnosis needs an in-person examination and usually a biopsy.

## Warning signs to get checked
See a dentist or doctor if any of these last **more than 2 weeks**:
- a mouth ulcer or sore that doesn't heal
- a white or red patch on the gums, tongue or lining of the mouth (leukoplakia or erythroplakia)
- a lump or thickening in the mouth, lip or neck
- unexplained bleeding or numbness, a loose tooth with no obvious cause, or difficulty swallowing

Most such changes turn out to be harmless, but they should always be examined.

## Risk factors
Tobacco in any form (including chewing tobacco, gutka and paan), heavy alcohol use, HPV infection, and sun exposure (for the lips). Risk rises with age.

## How screening works
At routine check-ups, dentists look at and feel the soft tissues of the mouth and neck. If something looks unusual they may review it after 2 weeks or refer to a specialist (oral surgeon or oral medicine) for a biopsy.
