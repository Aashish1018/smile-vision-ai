# Gum disease (gingivitis and periodontitis)

## What it is
**Gingivitis** is inflammation of the gums caused by plaque: red, puffy gums that may bleed when brushing. It is reversible. **Periodontitis** is more advanced: the supporting bone and attachment are lost, gums recede, pockets form, and teeth can loosen. Only a dentist can stage gum disease, using probing and X-rays.

## Who it suits
Treatment is recommended for anyone with bleeding, swollen or receding gums. Smokers and people with diabetes are at higher risk. Gum health should be stabilised **before** orthodontics, whitening or veneers.

## How it works
- **Professional cleaning** and better home care (brushing twice daily, interdental cleaning) for gingivitis.
- **Scaling and root planing** (deep cleaning) under local anaesthetic, usually by quadrant, for periodontitis.
- Ongoing periodontal maintenance every 3–4 months. Advanced cases may be referred to a periodontist.

## Timeline and results
Gingivitis often improves within 2 weeks of good care. Periodontitis can be controlled but bone loss is generally permanent.

## Risks and side effects
Temporary sensitivity and gum shrinkage after deep cleaning. Untreated periodontitis is a leading cause of tooth loss in adults.

## Cost factors
Number of quadrants needing deep cleaning, and the frequency of maintenance visits.
