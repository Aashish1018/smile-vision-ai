# Professional cleaning (scale and polish)

## What it is
A scale and polish removes hardened plaque (tartar or calculus) and surface stains that brushing cannot. Tartar often appears as yellow or brown deposits along the gum line, especially behind the lower front teeth.

## Who it suits
Almost everyone, usually every 6–12 months. It is often the first step before whitening, because removing surface stain and tartar can noticeably brighten teeth by itself and gives a true baseline shade.

## How it works
Ultrasonic and hand instruments remove deposits, then teeth are polished. Usually 30–45 minutes, with no anaesthetic needed for routine cleaning.

## Risks and side effects
Mild sensitivity or gum tenderness for a day or two.

## Cost factors
Usually a fixed fee. Heavy deposits may need a longer appointment or deep cleaning (see gum disease).
