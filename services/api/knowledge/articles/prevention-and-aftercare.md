# Everyday prevention and aftercare

## Daily habits
- Brush twice a day for two minutes with a fluoride toothpaste. Spit, don't rinse, so the fluoride stays on the teeth.
- Clean between teeth daily with floss or interdental brushes.
- Limit how often you have sugary food and drinks; frequency matters more than amount.
- Don't smoke or chew tobacco; both stain teeth and raise the risk of gum disease and mouth cancer.

## Check-ups
Visit a dentist at the interval they recommend, from every 3 months to every 2 years depending on your risk. Check-ups include a soft-tissue examination for early signs of mouth cancer.

## After cosmetic treatment
- **Whitening:** avoid strongly coloured food and drink for 48 hours; use a sensitivity toothpaste if needed.
- **Bonding and veneers:** don't bite hard objects; wear a night guard if you grind.
- **Aligners and braces:** wear retainers exactly as instructed, because teeth naturally drift back.
