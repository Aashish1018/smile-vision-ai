# Crowding, spacing and bite problems

## What they are
- **Crowding:** not enough room for teeth, so they overlap or twist. Crowded teeth are harder to clean and more prone to decay and gum disease.
- **Spacing:** gaps between teeth (a gap between the upper front teeth is called a diastema), from small teeth, missing teeth or habits.
- **Bite problems (malocclusion):** overbite, underbite, crossbite, open bite. Some are dental, some skeletal (jaw size or position).

## Options
- **Clear aligners** or **fixed braces** move teeth into better positions and address the cause.
- **Bonding** can close small gaps cosmetically without moving teeth.
- **Veneers** can mask mild irregularity, but they don't correct the bite.
- Significant skeletal problems may need orthodontics combined with jaw surgery (orthognathic surgery), planned with an orthodontist and maxillofacial surgeon.

## What a photo can and can't tell
A front smile photo can show visible crowding, gaps and some asymmetry. It cannot measure the bite, jaw relationships or root positions; those need a clinical exam, a 3D scan and often X-rays.
